# Masar - the Excel tab for planning engineers

Masar adds a **Masar** tab to Excel with tools for planning engineers working with Primavera P6 schedules (XER files):
WBS colours and roll-ups, weekly updates and dashboards, pre-submission checks, monthly report packs laid out by your
own template, delay events, delay analysis and time impact analysis, schedule comparison, S-curves, EVM, material
tracking and more. It works for any project and any client.

## Install (no administrator rights needed)

1. Open **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and download **Masar_Setup.zip**.
2. Right-click the zip > **Properties** > tick **Unblock** > OK. Then extract it.
3. Close Excel and double-click **Install_Masar.bat**.
4. Open Excel: the **Masar** tab is on the ribbon.

Works with Excel 2013 or newer on Windows (32-bit or 64-bit).

## Updates

When Excel starts (once a day) Masar checks this page for a newer version and offers it with its notes.
It is downloaded at once and installed when you close Excel. Masar tab > **Help and Feedback** >
**Check for Updates Now** checks straight away; **Automatic Update Check** turns the daily check off.
The check reads one small text file from this page and sends nothing about you or your files.

## Questions and suggestions

Masar tab > **Help and Feedback** > **Send a Suggestion** / **Ask a Question** / **Report a Problem** opens an
e-mail draft (with your Masar and Excel version) that you can edit before sending.

The publisher's contact details are in Masar: **Help and Feedback > About Masar**.
